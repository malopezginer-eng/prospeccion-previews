Questions

From your website or another public business listing. We use the information you publish to build the first version. Reply “remove” to any message and we delete your details and the draft the same day.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, with your written authorisation, and the page files are yours. If you stop the care plan, we hand everything over.

What if I want more than one page?

Most small businesses need exactly one good page. If you need more (a menu, a price list, a gallery), tell us and we quote it as a small add-on.
