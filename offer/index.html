Questions

How did you get my email and my details?

From your own website or public listing. The draft uses only public information about your business and stock photos; nothing was copied from your site. If you want the draft taken down, reply "remove" and it disappears the same day.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the page files are yours. If you stop the care plan, we hand everything over.

What if I want more than one page?

Most small businesses need exactly one good page. If you need more (a menu, a price list, a gallery), reply and we quote it as a small add-on.
