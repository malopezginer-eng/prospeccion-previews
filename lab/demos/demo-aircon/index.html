What we do

Every price below is per indoor unit and includes the drainage check and a written report.

  1. General servicing

    S$38 per unit

    Filters, blower wheel and drainage cleared, gas pressure read and logged. About 25 minutes per unit.

  2. Chemical wash

    S$95 per unit

    Coil and blower taken out and washed, drain line flushed. For units that smell or drip.

  3. Chemical overhaul

    S$180 per unit

    Indoor unit dismounted and washed off the wall, outdoor coil cleaned, full reassembly.

  4. Gas top-up and leak test

    from S$120

    Pressure test first. If it holds we top up; if it does not we quote the repair before touching it.

  5. Fault diagnosis

    S$60 call-out

    Not cooling, tripping or leaking. The call-out is waived if you go ahead with the repair.

  6. Installation and relocation

    Quoted on site

    New system fitted or an existing one moved, with piping, bracket and trunking quoted on site.

About Northvale Cooling Works

Northvale Cooling Works is a small servicing outfit: three two-person teams, a van each, and a workshop where units go when they need more than a wash. It covers flats, shophouse offices and small retail units.

Work is priced per indoor unit rather than per visit, so a three-room flat costs the same whichever technician turns up. Every job leaves a short written report with the gas pressure, what was cleaned and anything that looks close to failing, and the workmanship is covered for 90 days.

Questions people ask

How often should a unit be serviced?

Every three to four months for a home, more often in a kitchen or an office that runs all day.

My unit is dripping water. Is that a chemical wash?

Usually a blocked drain line, which a general service clears. If the coil is fouled as well, we quote the wash before starting.

Do you top up gas on the first visit?

Only after a pressure test holds. If there is a leak, topping up is money spent twice.

What does the 90-day warranty cover?

The workmanship on what we did. Parts carry whatever the manufacturer gives them, and that is written on the report.

Do I need to be home for the whole visit?

Someone has to let the team in and sign the report at the end. A three-unit flat is usually done in about ninety minutes.
