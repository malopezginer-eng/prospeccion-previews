What we do

Normal servicing

The regular clean: filters and covers washed, evaporator coil and drain pan checked, compressor pressures read, and the unit run before and after so you see the difference in airflow.

from S$35

Servicing contract

Three or four visits a year, booked ahead so nobody has to remember. Priced per number of fan coil units; condenser work is quoted separately.

from S$155 a year

Chemical overhaul

The deep clean for units that have stopped cooling properly. Parts come out and soak, and the price steps up with unit size: 9k/12k, 18k and 24k BTU are all listed separately.

from S$130

Repair and parts replacement

Troubleshooting and spare parts for wall units and ceiling ducted systems, including fan coil insulation, ducted filters and blower cleaning. Three months warranty on parts fitted.

Gas top-up and pressure testing

R22 and R410A top-ups, plus pressure tests on one to four fan coil systems and on ceiling ducted units when a leak is suspected rather than guessed at.

from S$40

Condenser and cassette work

Gas flushing or chemical wash of the outdoor condenser, chemical wash and overhaul for ceiling cassettes, plus fan coil relocation and swaps.

from S$50

About CC Air-con Engineering

CC Air-con Engineering has been servicing air conditioners in Singapore since October 2016, working out of Woodlands. The technicians are Mandarin-speaking and the company sells itself on turning up on time and telling you plainly what a unit needs, rather than upselling the biggest job on the list.

Everything is priced per fan coil unit and published as a table, from a S$35 single-unit service up to ceiling ducted work, so a quote is a matter of counting units. Spare part replacements carry a three-month warranty, and so does water leaking after a service or overhaul.

Questions people ask

How much is a normal service?

S$35 for one unit and S$55 for two, rising to S$152 for eight units in one visit.

Is a servicing contract worth it?

It works out cheaper per visit and the slots are booked ahead. Three visits a year for three units is S$185; four visits is S$220. Condenser work is not included.

Do you work on ceiling ducted and cassette units?

Yes, including chemical wash and overhaul for cassettes, ducted filter changes and pressure testing on ducted systems.

What warranty do I get?

Three months on any spare part replaced, and three months on water leaking after a servicing or chemical overhaul.
