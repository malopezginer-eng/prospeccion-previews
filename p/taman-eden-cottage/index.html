What we do

Bungalow for two

Each of the four bungalows has a queen canopy bed, air conditioning and a ceiling fan, a private terrace, safe, minibar, desk, wardrobe and free WiFi. Minimum stay is two nights.

Breakfast, your choice

Breakfast is included every morning and you pick between continental, American or French.

Pool and garden

A swimming pool with loungers, umbrellas and towels, set in the garden with the village and the rice fields around it.

Massage on site

Massage can be arranged at the cottage, so you do not have to go into Ubud for it.

Airport transfer

Pick-up from Denpasar airport, 31 km away, can be booked in advance for an extra fee.

Scooter and driver

Scooter hire and a driver for day trips can be arranged from the property for an extra fee.

About Taman Eden Cottage

Taman Eden Cottage is a small guesthouse in Bentuyung, a village on Jalan Kelabang Moding just north of central Ubud. There are four bungalows and one pool, so the place never holds more than a handful of guests at a time.

The hosts speak French, English and Indonesian, and run it as a stay rather than a hotel: breakfast cooked to order, massage arranged on site, a scooter or a driver organised when you want to go further out. The Monkey Forest, Ubud Palace and the Tegallalang rice terraces are all short rides away, and the Pyramids of Chi meditation centre is a walk down the lane.
