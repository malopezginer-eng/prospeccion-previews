About Niramaya Villa

Niramaya Villa is a small place on Jalan Arjuna, down Gang Raja in Seminyak. Two units, one on the ground floor and one upstairs, both looking onto the same pool and garden. It is a residential lane, so nights are quiet, but Double Six Beach is a ten-minute walk and central Kuta a fifteen-minute drive.

The villa is run like a home rather than a hotel. Rooms are serviced daily, coffee, tea and drinking water are refilled, and the kitchen means you can cook instead of eating out every night. The same staff arrange airport pick-ups, a driver for the day or a car, and can bring breakfast or a massage to the villa. For longer stays, ask about the monthly rate.

Questions people ask

How far is the beach?

Double Six Beach is about a ten-minute walk. Central Kuta is a fifteen-minute drive and the airport about thirty minutes.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is not part of the rate, but it can be served in your villa on request. Coffee, tea and drinking water are refilled daily at no charge.

Do you do monthly rates?

Yes. Rates depend on the dates, and there is a discounted rate for stays of thirty nights or more. Email or call with your dates.

Can you pick us up at the airport?

Yes. Airport transfers and shuttle services are arranged by the villa and charged separately.
