About Guildwood Villa Bali

Guildwood Villa is a guest house on Jalan Pengembak in Sanur, part of the Anteng Villa Group. Three suites, six guests at most, furnished with a mix of Indonesian and European pieces, around a pool with lounge chairs and a billiard room. It sits on a quiet residential lane, 350 metres from the beach and away from the traffic.

It is built to be small on purpose. With three rooms you get privacy when you want it and company when you feel like it, and the same people look after you whether you stay one night or a year. Sanur's main street, Jalan Tamblingan, runs the length of the beach with shops, restaurants and cafes playing acoustic music in the evenings, and Seminyak, Canggu, Kuta and Legian are all an easy drive when you want a louder night out.

Questions people ask

What does Room 1 cost?

IDR 695,000 per night for one or two guests, taxes included. An extra guest on a third mattress is IDR 200,000 per night. Ask us for the current rates on Rooms 2 and 3.

Can I stay for months rather than nights?

Yes. Suites are available on 3, 6, 9 or 12 month leases at IDR 15,000,000 per month per suite, all taxes included, with terms set up to match Indonesian long-stay visa requirements.

How far is the beach and the main street?

The beach is 350 metres away. Jalan Tamblingan, Sanur's main street with the shops, restaurants and cafes, runs along the beach and is a short walk from the house.

Do you sort out food, laundry and transport?

Yes. Breakfasts and meals, an economical laundry service, drinks at any hour, in-house massage, bicycle and motorbike hire, a car with driver and guided day tours are all arranged here.
