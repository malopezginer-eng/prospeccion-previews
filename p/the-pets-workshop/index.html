About The Pets Workshop

The Pets Workshop opened in 2021 in a small shop in Tampines. The flagship is now at Telepark, split across two units: dogs in one, cats, rabbits and guinea pigs in the other. There are three more shops, at Serangoon NEX, Clarke Quay and Kovan.

The cat room is the part people talk about. It has no dogs in it and a glass front, so you can stand and watch your cat being groomed instead of handing it over and hoping. The stylists carry Singapore Kennel Club and international grooming certifications, and the same team runs a grooming academy for owners and for people training into the trade.

Questions people ask

Do you groom cats?

Yes, in a dedicated room at Telepark with no dogs around. WhatsApp the cat line on +65 8499 5160 to book.

Do you take rabbits and guinea pigs?

Yes. Both have their own basic and full grooming prices, starting at S$40 for rabbits and S$35 for guinea pigs.

Can I book just a nail trim?

Yes. The mix and match list lets you pick single items such as nail clipping, ear cleaning or tooth brushing without a full groom.

Do you teach grooming?

Yes. The academy runs a three-hour basic dog grooming course for owners aged 18 and over, who bring their own dog, plus professional courses. Ask on +65 8869 5412.
