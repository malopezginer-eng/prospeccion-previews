About The Pets Workshop

The Pets Workshop started in Tampines in 2021. The Tampines shop now sits in Telepark, split across two units: dogs in #01-10, cats, rabbits and guinea pigs next door in #01-12. Three more outlets followed, at Serangoon NEX, Clarke Quay and Kovan.

The cat side is the part owners talk about. It has no dogs in it and a see-through glass wall, so you can stand and watch the groom instead of handing your cat over and hoping. The stylists hold Singapore Kennel Club and international grooming certifications, and the same team runs an academy that trains owners and new groomers.

What we do

Prices are the published ranges from their services page. Dog rates are split into small, medium and large, and the workshop does not publish the weight brackets behind those sizes; the final price depends on the pet and the coat. Spa treatments and add-ons are charged separately.

  1. Express bath

    from S$25

    A wash and blow dry when the coat is fine and the dog just needs cleaning up. Priced by size, small through to large breeds.

  2. Basic grooming

    from S$35

    Nail clipping and filing, ear cleaning, brushing and combing, paw pad clipping, sanitary trim, anal gland expression, then bath and dry. For dogs, cats, rabbits and guinea pigs.

  3. Full grooming

    from S$65

    The basic groom plus a full trim and styling of the coat. Small dogs up to large breeds, and also cats, rabbits and guinea pigs.

  4. Cat grooming at the Cat Sanctuary

    from S$65

    A separate unit at Telepark with no dogs in it, walled with see-through glass so you can watch the whole groom. Basic and full grooms, plus a spa made for cats.

  5. Spa treatments

    from S$7

    Herbal, sensitive and irritable skin care, standard skin and fur treatment, CO2 and a paw pad spa. Added to a groom or booked on its own.

  6. Mix and match add-ons

    from S$7

    Pick only what you need: nail clipping or filing, tooth brushing, tartar removal, ear cleaning or ear hair plucking, poodle feet and face, head trim, de-matting and undercoat removal.

Why people choose The Pets Workshop

Cats have their own room

The Cat Sanctuary is a separate unit with no dogs in it, so there is no barking and no dog dander while your cat is groomed.

You can watch through the glass

The cat room is walled with see-through glass, so the groom happens in front of you rather than out the back.

Certified groomers, and their own academy

The stylists hold Singapore Kennel Club and international certifications, and the shop runs grooming courses for owners and for people training into the trade.

Questions people ask

Do you groom cats?

Yes, in the Cat Sanctuary at Telepark: a separate unit with no dogs in it and a glass wall you can watch through. WhatsApp the cat line on +65 8499 5160.

Do you take rabbits and guinea pigs?

Yes. Both have their own basic and full grooming prices, from S$40 for rabbits and S$35 for guinea pigs.

Can I book just a nail trim?

Yes. The mix and match list lets you pick single items such as nail clipping, ear cleaning or tooth brushing without booking a full groom.

Do you teach grooming?

Yes. The academy runs a three-hour basic dog grooming course for owners aged 18 and over who bring their own dog, plus professional courses. Ask on +65 8869 5412.
