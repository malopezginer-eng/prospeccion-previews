About Knowledge Kindler Learning Centre

Knowledge Kindler sits on the ground floor of Aperia at 12 Kallang Avenue, a six-minute walk from Lavender MRT. It was set up by former school heads of department and classroom teachers, and it takes children from K2 through Secondary, with Chemistry going up to Junior College.

Classes are capped at ten. The teaching leans on inquiry, Socratic questioning and storytelling rather than worksheets alone, and the centre is open about wanting children to do well in exams and still enjoy the subject. Centre Director Alvin Ang taught for 17 years and was a Science HOD and school staff developer; Centre Manager Yvonne Cheak has 14 years in Physics and Maths and a specialist diploma in specific learning differences from DAS. During the school holidays the centre also runs short STEM, Chinese and coding sessions.
