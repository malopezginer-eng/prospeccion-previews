About Grease Monkey Barber Garage

Grease Monkey opened on Mayo Street in Rochor in 2015 and has stayed a no-frills barbershop: no lounge, no ceremony, just barbers who cut well. The owner got into barbering after walking into Crowsnest Barbershop in Toronto in 2011 while touring with a band, and the shop still leans hard on classic American barbering — bald fades, tapers, 360 waves.

The bench is deep, with taper and texture specialists among the barbers, and the shop has built a name for curly and textured hair as well as colour work. Bring an open mind: if your hair type wants something different from what you asked for, they will say so. A second shop opened at 22 Bali Lane, a few minutes away, with the same feel.
