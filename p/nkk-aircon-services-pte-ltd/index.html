What we do

Aircon servicing

Routine servicing of fan coils and outdoor condensers, drainage cleared and the system checked over. Booked as a one-off or on a schedule.

Chemical bath and cleaning

The deeper clean for units where a normal service no longer restores the cooling or clears a smell. Parts are treated properly rather than sprayed over.

Repairs and fault finding

Water leaking down the wall, a unit blowing warm, a system that trips. The fault is traced first, then the repair is quoted.

Gas refuelling

Refrigerant top-up when a unit runs but no longer cools, after checking that the gas is actually the problem.

Installation

Supply and fitting of new systems, working across Daikin, Panasonic, Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu, Toshiba, LG, Midea and Gree.

Servicing contracts

Household packages for flats and houses, and maintenance contracts for commercial and industrial sites. Their client list runs from an international school and the SPCA to hotpot restaurants and a shopping centre.

About NKK Aircon Services

NKK works out of its own unit at 1014 Geylang East Avenue 3, #01-206, in the Geylang East Industrial Estate. The business was registered with ACRA in April 2011 as NKK Air-Con Services and incorporated as NKK Air Con Pte Ltd in August 2015; both are live and both sit at the same address. It is a family-run outfit and it says so on its own site.

The work covers the whole range: install, service, chemical clean, repair, gas top-up and the awkward jobs like water leaking or a unit that runs hot. Domestic and commercial sit side by side on the books, and the named clients include Anglo-Chinese School (International), the SPCA, Pet Lovers Centre, Orchard Shopping Centre and NCS Hub. Four phone lines are published, two of them mobiles, so someone usually picks up.
