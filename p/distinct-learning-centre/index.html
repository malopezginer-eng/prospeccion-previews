About Distinct Learning Centre

Distinct Student Care and Enrichment Centre has been running from Blk 107 Serangoon North Avenue 1, unit #01-681, since 2011. It is a ground-floor void deck unit, a short walk from the flats and schools around Serangoon North.

The centre appears on the Ministry of Social and Family Development's published list of student care centres, with a monthly fee of S$400 as at March 2025. Its registered activity is child and student care for school-going children, with enrichment and training courses alongside.
