About Car 888 Auto Services

Car 888 Auto Services is an independent workshop on the second floor of AMK Auto Point, at 10 Ang Mo Kio Industrial Park 2A. It is a repair shop first: gearbox work, automatic transmission overhauls, engine overhauls and ECU programming, with normal servicing alongside.

Bring the car in and the technicians look at it for free before quoting, so you find out what is actually wrong before you commit to anything. Everyday jobs are covered too, from a battery or tyre change to oil and brake checks, and there is a breakdown call-out.
