What we do

Blocked drains and chokes

Floor traps, basins and kitchen sinks cleared. The usual 3am call, and the reason the line is answered around the clock.

Leaking pipes

Repair or replacement of leaking water and waste pipework, in flats, condos and landed property.

Taps, mixers and cistern fittings

Replacement of faucets, water taps and mixers, plus flushing systems and toilet seat covers. They stock or source most brands, Grohe and Hansgrohe through to Saniton and Johnson Suisse.

Sanitary ware supply and install

Toilet bowls, basins, bidets, hand sprays and bathroom accessories supplied and fitted, including converting a squat pan to a sitting toilet bowl.

Water heaters

Supply and installation of instant heaters and storage heaters.

Rubbish chutes

New aluminium or stainless steel refuse chutes and dustbins supplied and installed.

About Ocean Plumbing

Ocean Plumbing is a Singapore plumbing company, registered here since January 2020, working islandwide on HDB flats, condominiums and landed property. The service runs 24 hours, which is the part that matters when a floor trap backs up on a public holiday.

Pricing is done job by job. They look at the problem first, quote a figure, and only start once you have agreed it, and the repair work carries a warranty. Calls and SMS both go to the same number.
