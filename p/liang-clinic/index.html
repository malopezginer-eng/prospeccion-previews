What we do

Everyday illness

Consultation and treatment for coughs, fevers and infections, with injections and nebuliser therapy on site when they are needed.

Chronic disease care

Ongoing management of high blood pressure, diabetes, high cholesterol and asthma, with the same doctor following the case rather than a different face each visit.

Children and vaccinations

Childhood immunisations on schedule plus developmental assessments, so the whole family can be seen in one place.

Women's health

Family planning advice, breast examination and PAP smears in a general practice setting, without a hospital referral.

Men's health

Erectile dysfunction, premature ejaculation and testosterone deficiency, plus confidential STD screening and treatment.

Screening, medicals and travel jabs

Health screening, vocational medicals for work permits, driving licences and employment, advance medical directives, and travel vaccinations for hepatitis A and B, typhoid and influenza. Minor procedures such as removing lumps and bumps, draining abscesses and wound care are done in the clinic.

About Liang Clinic

Liang Clinic is a general practice in the basement of Siglap Centre, 55 Siglap Road, right next to the escalator. It moved into the unit in February 2019 after years on East Coast Road, and it serves Siglap and Frankel Estate. The doctor is Dr Justin Leong.

It works the way a neighbourhood clinic is supposed to: one doctor who sees the children for their jabs, the parents for a screening and the grandparents for a blood pressure review. CHAS and Pioneer Generation cards are handled at the counter, and the clinic lists a set of health insurance plans it accepts. Open six days a week with a break over lunch, and closed on Sundays.

Questions people ask

Where is the clinic?

55 Siglap Road, #B1-15/16 Siglap Centre, Singapore 455871, on the basement level next to the escalator.

Do I need to book?

Call 6241 5944 to arrange a time. Consultations run 8:30 to 12:00 and 14:00 to 17:00 on weekdays, and 8:30 to 12:00 on Saturday.

Do you take CHAS or my company insurance?

CHAS and Pioneer Generation cards are accepted. The clinic also publishes the health plans it is on; if yours is not on the list, call and ask.

Can I get a work permit medical or travel vaccinations here?

Yes. Vocational screening for work permits, driving licences and employment is done in the clinic, along with hepatitis A and B, typhoid and influenza vaccinations.
