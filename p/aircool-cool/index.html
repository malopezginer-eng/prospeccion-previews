What we do

Prices as published by AirCool & Cool. Ask for a quote on anything not listed.

  1. Standard servicing and contract packages

    from S$40

    Filter and coil cleaning, drainage pan clearing, blower fan check and a pressure test on the compressor. Book it one-off or as a three or four times a year contract.

  2. Chemical wash

    from S$80

    Filters, evaporator and condenser coils come off and soak in chemical solution to shift acid stains and mould. Drainage is vacuumed out and the unit is deodorised.

  3. Chemical overhaul

    from S$140

    The full strip-down for units that no longer cool, drip water or rattle. Every part is dismantled, cleaned, checked for damage and the fan bearings are lubricated.

  4. Gas top-up

    from S$50

    Leak check first, then a refill of R22, R410A or R32 depending on your unit. Usually done straight after a service when the cooling has dropped off.

  5. Troubleshooting and part replacement

    from S$40

    Unit not powering on, thermostat playing up, or a part that needs swapping. Most faults are sorted within the hour; parts that need ordering take a day or so.

  6. Installation and VRV maintenance

    New unit installation for flats, houses and commercial premises, plus chemical condenser work on VRV systems. Quoted after a look at the site.

About AirCool & Cool

AirCool & Cool is a Punggol aircon outfit, registered in Singapore in July 2017 and run out of Punggol Breeze. They are family owned and operated, and they work on both homes and commercial premises across the island rather than just the north-east.

The work is priced per unit and quoted before the appointment is confirmed, so you know what a three-unit chemical wash costs before anyone turns up. They handle Daikin, Mitsubishi, Panasonic, Fujitsu, Midea, Toshiba and Samsung, and every visit ends with a diagnostic report on what was found.

Questions people ask

What does a normal service cost?

S$40 for one unit, S$50 for two, S$65 for three, S$80 for four, S$95 for five and S$115 for six.

What is the difference between a chemical wash and a chemical overhaul?

A chemical wash cleans the coils and filters in place. An overhaul dismantles the whole unit, so it is the one to book when the aircon has stopped cooling or is leaking water.

Do you cover areas outside Punggol?

Yes. The base is at Punggol Breeze but the team works across Singapore, residential and commercial.

Which refrigerant do I need for a top-up?

It depends on your unit. R22 runs from S$50, R410A from S$80 and R32 from S$120; the technician confirms which one on site.
