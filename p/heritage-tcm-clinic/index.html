About Heritage TCM Clinic

Heritage TCM Clinic has been at Lucky Plaza since 2012, on the fifth floor in the Orchard Medical Specialists Centre. Patients describe it as a quiet room in the middle of one of the busiest stretches of Singapore, which is more or less the idea. It is the clinic arm of HST Medical, a Singapore health company with a long history in traditional Chinese medicine.

Consultations are with physician Tan Shiau Tse, a graduate of Nanjing University of Chinese Medicine and the Singapore College of Traditional Chinese Medicine, registered with the Traditional Chinese Medicine Practitioners Board and practising in English and Mandarin. The work is mostly pain, stress, insomnia, anxiety and fatigue, digestive and sinus complaints, skin conditions such as eczema, sports injuries, and women's health and fertility. Patients who were nervous about needles tend to mention afterwards that they barely felt them.
