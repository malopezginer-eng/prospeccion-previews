What we do

Normal servicing

Air filters, cooling coil and condenser coil cleaned, drainage pipe vacuumed, drain pan and blower cleaned, then the system is run and tested before he leaves.

Chemical servicing

Chemical treatment of the fan coils and a chemical wash, plus the drainage pipe and blower, with a test run at the end. The step up when a normal service no longer brings the cooling back.

Household visits by unit count

Slots are booked by how many fan coils you have, one through five, and the price is fixed before the technician arrives. One hour per visit.

About H&C Air-con Service

H&C Air-con Service is a small Singapore operation that has been servicing homes since 2010, registered with ACRA at the end of December 2009. The proprietor has spent more than thirty years in air-conditioning, and he is the one who turns up. Registration 53158425M and licence SSA000458 are published openly on the site rather than hidden.

The work is residential and deliberately simple: normal servicing or chemical servicing, priced by how many fan coils you have, so you know the bill before anyone rings the bell. One unit is S$50 and five units S$150, with a flat S$150 for larger BTU units. Weekdays run 9am to 6pm and Saturdays to 4pm; Sundays and public holidays are possible for S$10 more. Booking is by phone, email, Facebook or Carousell.

Questions people ask

How much is a service?

One unit is S$50, two S$80, three S$100, four S$120 and five S$150. A larger BTU unit is S$150. Each slot is an hour.

Can you come on a Sunday or a public holiday?

Yes, by arrangement, with a S$10 surcharge on top of the usual rate. Weekdays are 9am to 6pm and Saturday 9am to 4pm.

What is the difference between normal and chemical servicing?

Normal servicing cleans the filters, coils, drain pan, blower and drainage pipe. Chemical servicing adds a chemical treatment of the fan coils and a chemical wash, for units that a normal service no longer revives.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Business registration 53158425M, registered since December 2009, and licence number SSA000458, both published on the site.
