About Athena Bali Villa

Athena Bali Villa is a one-villa rental on Jalan Gunung Athena, inside the Bali Griya Residence complex in West Denpasar. It is a residential street, so it stays quiet, but Seminyak Beach, Double Six, Petitenget and Batu Belig are all about ten minutes away by car, and the airport around thirty.

The whole place is yours: a private pool, a full kitchen, a living room with a Smart TV, air conditioning, laundry and fast WiFi. A minimart and a laundry shop are a two-minute walk away, and Sunset Point mall about five minutes by car. It suits two people staying a few nights, a month or longer, and check-in is self-service so late arrivals are not a problem.

Questions people ask

How many people can stay?

The villa is set up for two guests, with one queen bed. Children are not allowed and extra beds are not available.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out until 11:00. Check-in is self-service, so let us know roughly when you will arrive.

How far is the beach and the airport?

About ten minutes by car to Seminyak Beach, Double Six, Petitenget and Batu Belig, and around thirty minutes to Ngurah Rai airport and to Canggu.

How do I get the rate for my dates?

Message us on WhatsApp at +62 817 470 4718 or email athenabalivilla@gmail.com with your dates. Daily, monthly and yearly rates are available.
