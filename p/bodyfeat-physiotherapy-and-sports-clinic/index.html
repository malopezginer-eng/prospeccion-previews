What we do

Physiotherapy, 60 minutes

The session every new patient starts with. Full history of the symptoms and how the injury happened, then testing of strength, joint range and stability before any treatment.

Physiotherapy, 45 minutes

Follow-up sessions once the picture is clear. Hands-on work and progression of the exercise plan, with the home programme updated each visit.

Manual therapy and dry needling

Soft tissue release, joint mobilisations and needling used inside a session where they help, rather than as a treatment on their own.

Strength and conditioning, 60 minutes

Coaching for athletes on strength, power, speed and game conditioning. If something still hurts, they will send you to a physiotherapist first.

Sports massage, 60 minutes

For tightness and post-training soreness. You usually leave with a couple of stretches and light exercises to do at home.

About BodyFEAT Physiotherapy and Sports Clinic

BodyFEAT opened in 2020 on the fifth floor of SBF Center on Robinson Road, a few minutes from Tanjong Pagar MRT. It is a small allied health team treating musculoskeletal injuries and sports injuries, mostly for office workers and for people who train seriously outside work.

The founder and principal physiotherapist is Quah Wee Heng, who trained at Auckland University of Technology and holds NKT Level 3. He has competed in indoor and beach volleyball, dragon boat and triathlon, and works from the view that pain usually traces back to a movement problem somewhere else. Cheryl Germaine Low runs the rehabilitation training side with a sports science degree from NTU and a background coaching netball and youth athletics; ankles and knees are her area. New patients are booked into a full hour so nothing gets rushed.

How long is the first appointment?

Can I book strength and conditioning if I am injured?

Speak to a physiotherapist first. If pain is limiting you, the clinic will start there and move you into conditioning once it is safe.
