About Florence Music

Florence Music has been teaching on Level 6 of Parkway Centre, at 1 Marine Parade Central, since 2009. The school has more than fifteen studios there and covers piano, violin, guitar, ukulele, voice and music theory, plus piano accompaniment for instrumentalists. It is one of the ABRSM examination centres in Singapore.

Nearly everything is taught one to one, once a week, in 30, 45 or 60-minute lessons, with the day and time set to fit the student. The school takes beginners from four years old and uses the Australian Encore ON Keys method with the youngest ones, on the view that musicianship should start early rather than waiting for a child to be big enough for an instrument. Alongside lessons there are performances, study classes, workshops, camps and master-classes through the year. The principal is Ms Florence FL Lim.

Questions people ask

How young can my child start?

Piano and violin take beginners from four years old. Guitar and voice start at six.

How long is a lesson?

Weekly individual lessons of 30, 45 or 60 minutes depending on the instrument, with the day and time arranged to suit you.

What do I pay to get started?

A one-time registration fee of S$40 and a S$150 deposit, refundable if you give one month's notice and all fees are settled. Monthly lesson fees are quoted at the front counter.

Do you prepare students for exams?

Yes. The school trains students for music examinations and is one of the ABRSM examination centres in Singapore.
