What we do

First visit, adults and teens

Consultation, examination, a read of any X-rays or MRI you bring, the findings explained, and the first treatment. Allow 45 to 60 minutes.

from S$170

Follow-up visit

A 15 to 20 minute treatment session. Most courses of care run somewhere between 4 and 24 visits depending on how long the problem has been there.

from S$115

Children, 12 and under

Assessment and treatment for children, at a lower first-visit and follow-up rate. The clinic has treated patients from babies to people in their nineties.

from S$120

Back, neck and disc problems

Slipped disc, disc bulge and prolapse, cervical and lumbar spondylosis, lumbar stenosis, pain between the shoulder blades, numbness and tingling, headaches and migraine, and back pain in pregnancy.

Sports and limb injuries

Shoulder injuries, tennis and golfer's elbow, wrist, hand, knee, ankle and foot pain. Injury management, prevention and performance work for athletes at any level, by a chiropractor certified in sports practice.

Scoliosis and wellness care

Scoliosis is managed with a mixed plan — adjustment, exercise, physical therapy, bracing and orthopaedic input — rather than adjustment alone. Wellness care is periodic maintenance plus advice on posture, load and lifestyle.

About Elder Chiropractic Clinic

Elder Chiropractic Clinic opened in 1978, started by Carol S. Elder-Birnbaum. It is the oldest chiropractic practice in Singapore and one of the oldest in Southeast Asia, and it has treated more than 25,000 people since — babies through to patients in their nineties. The clinic sits at #14-03 in ARC 380, at the Lavender end of Jalan Besar.

Janet Ruth Sosna, DC, CCSP, joined in 1985 and is now the senior chiropractor and clinic director. She is a naturalised Singaporean, has held teaching posts with chiropractic institutions in the United States, Australia and Malaysia, was a founding member and past president of The Chiropractic Association (Singapore), and led the Asia-Pacific Chiropractic Doctors' Federation from 2007 to 2015. The clinic works case by case with other doctors when a problem needs more than chiropractic, and it does not sell discounted pre-paid packages.

Questions people ask

What does a first visit cost?

S$170 for adults and teens on a weekday, S$120 for children 12 and under. Saturday first visits are S$250 and are only taken in exceptional cases, because Saturday is the busiest day. Follow-ups are S$115, or S$90 for children.

How many visits will I need?

Typically somewhere between 4 and 24 visits over a similar number of weeks, depending on the condition. The clinic does not sell discounted pre-paid packages.

How do I get an appointment?

Call +65 6235 9843 or +65 6734 8584. You can also text or WhatsApp +65 9656 8734, or email ElderChiroSG@yahoo.com — but online messages may not be picked up until the next working day, so call if it is urgent.
