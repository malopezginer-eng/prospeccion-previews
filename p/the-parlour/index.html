About The Parlour

The Parlour is a small hair studio on Lowland Road, a two-minute walk from Kovan MRT. It was started by Jessi, an American stylist who qualified in 2003 and has worked in Chicago, New York, San Francisco and Singapore. Expat Living readers have voted her one of the city's best hairdressers.

The team is trained across several countries, so hair that behaves differently gets treated differently. It is an official Kevin Murphy salon and stocks the full range, with Olaplex for repair and Bellami for extensions. Colour correction and blonde work are the jobs people travel across the island for. The diary fills weeks ahead, so book early.
