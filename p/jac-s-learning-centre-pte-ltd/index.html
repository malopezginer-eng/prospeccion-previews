What we do

Primary Maths

Small-group coaching from lower primary through PSLE, built around the topics children actually lose marks on. Taught by tutors with NIE teaching diplomas.

Secondary Maths

E-Maths and A-Maths up to O-Levels, including a dedicated trigonometry track. One tutor also takes Junior College maths.

Science

Primary and Secondary Science with Mrs Doris Lim, a former MOE teacher who has written primary science assessment material and concept maps.

English

Primary and Secondary English, including O-Level and IGCSE candidates. Focus on comprehension, writing and exam technique.

Humanities

History, Geography and Social Studies for Secondary students, with model answers and source-based question practice.

Coding and robotics

Gummy Code classes using LEGO WeDo, EV3 and SPIKE, plus Scratch and Python. Project-based, so children finish with something that works.

About Jac's Learning Centre

Jac's Learning Centre opened in 2008 at Canberra Community Club in Sembawang, back when it was still called Jelutung CC. It was founded by Jackie S.K. Lim, who read Computational Mathematics at NUS and holds a postgraduate diploma in education from NIE. The centre has stayed in the same building ever since, at 2 Sembawang Crescent.

What began as maths and science tuition now covers English, humanities, coding and robotics. Nine tutors teach here, several of them former MOE teachers, and classes are kept small so each child gets attention. The centre deliberately does not publish students' results, on the view that the work belongs to the child rather than the centre.
