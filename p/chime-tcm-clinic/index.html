About CHIME TCM Clinic

CHIME opened in Teck Whye in 2021. Sean Lee started it after years in both public and private practice: he came through the double degree in Biomedical Sciences and Chinese Medicine at NTU and Beijing University of Chinese Medicine, has been practising since 2012, set up the TCM traumatology unit at the NTU Chinese Medicine Clinic and worked at Kwong Wai Shiu Hospital. He is registered with the Ministry of Health and mentors trainee physicians.

There are three registered physicians now. Hong Simin came through the same NTU and Beijing double degree route. Cecilia Lim Huimin trained at Guangzhou University of Chinese Medicine and works on pelvic floor disorders, women's health and postpartum recovery. Between them they cover acute and chronic complaints, men's and women's health, children's wellness, skin conditions, pain and injury. Prices are on the website, which is rarer than it should be, and the clinic will talk to you about corporate wellness and insurance claims.
