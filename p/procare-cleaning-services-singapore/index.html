What we do

Sofa cleaning

Fabric sofas cleaned in place with a water-based vacuum system and a high-pressure jet spray, so the dirt is pulled out rather than pushed deeper.

Mattress cleaning

Deep cleaning for mattresses and bed frames, aimed at what builds up in a bed over the years in a hot, humid climate.

Carpet and rug cleaning

Carpets and rugs cleaned on site, at home or in the office, without sending them away.

Curtain cleaning

Curtains cleaned where they hang, with the method matched to the fabric. Nothing has to come down, so there is no bare-window day.

Post-renovation and deep cleaning

The clean after the contractors leave, plus general deep cleaning and disinfection of a whole home or unit.

Floor polishing and restoration

Parquet and hard floors sanded and polished back to a proper finish.

About Procare Cleaning Services Singapore

Procare Cleaning Services is a Singapore cleaning company working out of Vertex Tower B on Ubi Avenue 3. Its speciality is soft furnishings: sofas, mattresses, carpets, dining chairs, bed frames and curtains, cleaned in the home rather than taken away.

The work is done with water-based vacuum equipment and a high-pressure jet spray, and the same team also handles post-renovation cleaning, disinfection and floor polishing. Alongside private homes it takes on corporate and hospitality jobs, and it answers the phone seven days a week.

Questions people ask

Do you take the sofa or curtains away?

No. Upholstery and curtains are cleaned in place, so there is nothing to remove and nothing to wait for.

What do you use to clean?

A water-based vacuum system with a high-pressure jet spray, together with their own cleaning solution.

Do you work on weekends?

Yes. They operate Monday to Sunday, 9am to 7pm.

Do you take on office and commercial jobs?

Yes, as well as homes. They have done cleaning for hotel and corporate clients.
