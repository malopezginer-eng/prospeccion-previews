About He'enalu Cafe & Resto Uluwatu

He'enalu sits on Jalan Raya Uluwatu in Pecatu, near Nirmala supermarket and within a short ride of Padang Padang, Bingin, Dreamland, Suluban and Balangan. It has been feeding surfers and their families on the Bukit since 2017.

The kitchen is Hawaiian at heart and cooks from scratch each day: poke bowls, loco moco and huli-huli chicken alongside brunch plates, pasta, burgers and a vegan section that is properly marked rather than tacked on. Air-conditioned inside, open for dine-in and takeaway, and delivering to addresses within four kilometres.

Questions people ask

Are you open on Saturdays?

No. He'enalu opens Sunday to Friday, 11:00 to 19:30, and closes on Saturdays.

Do you deliver?

Yes, direct delivery to addresses within about 4 km. Last takeaway orders are taken at 19:00.

Are the menu prices final?

Menu prices are before 10% tax and a 5% service charge.

Is there anything for vegans?

Yes, a full vegan section with items marked (V) and (GF), including bowls, sandwiches, wraps and fried rice.
