What we do

General servicing

Filters, evaporator coils and drainage cleaned, condenser coils, electrical contacts and fan bearings checked, drain pans vacuumed and compressor pressures measured. They suggest every three to four months at home, monthly for commercial units.

from S$50

Chemical wash and chemical overhaul

Electrical contacts and refrigerant piping disconnected, fan coil, cooling coils and water trays taken out and cleaned with a chemical solution, then reassembled and tested. For units a normal service no longer revives.

from S$90

Yearly servicing contract

Three or four scheduled visits a year covering the indoor fan coil, the outdoor condenser and the drainage piping, with transport charges waived for the whole contract period.

from S$170

Checking and troubleshooting

A technician diagnoses the fault, explains what he found and quotes the repair. The call-out covers transport and checking, and it is waived if you go ahead with the job. Replaced parts carry 90 days.

S$50

Installation and replacement

Site assessment first, then the right model chosen for the room and installed properly, so the manufacturer's warranty stays intact. Maintenance packages afterwards if you want them.

About Ideal Aircon Engineering

Ideal Aircon Engineering runs service teams across Singapore from a unit at 9 Yishun Industry Street 1, #02-66. It works on homes, offices and shops, and on non-profit premises, with supervisors holding trade qualifications and technicians trained to the servicing standards. The office runs Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm, and the line stays open around the clock for emergencies.

What sets the company apart in a trade full of on-the-spot quotes is that the numbers are published. General servicing runs from S$50 for one fan coil to S$135 for six. A chemical wash is S$90 to S$150 per fan coil and a chemical overhaul S$120 to S$180, with wall-mounted units priced by BTU. Yearly contracts start at S$170 for two fan coils and include the transport waiver. Diagnosis is S$50, refunded against the repair. Brands covered include Daikin, Mitsubishi, Fujitsu, Hitachi, LG, Samsung, Panasonic and Carrier.

Questions people ask

How much is a standard service?

One fan coil S$50, two S$65, three S$85, four S$100, five S$115 and six S$135, as a one-time visit.

What does a yearly contract cost?

Three visits a year: S$170 for two fan coils, S$210 for three, S$250 for four, S$330 for five, S$350 for six. Four visits a year: S$210, S$240, S$310, S$380 and S$430. Transport is waived throughout the contract.

When do I need a chemical wash instead of a normal service?

When cleaning no longer restores the cooling or clears a smell. A chemical wash is S$90 to S$150 a fan coil and an overhaul S$120 to S$180. For wall-mounted units, 9,000 to 12,000 BTU is S$120 to S$150 and 18,000 to 24,000 BTU is S$150 to S$180.

What if the fault turns out to be something else?

The S$50 checking fee covers transport and diagnosis and is waived if you confirm the repair. Any part replaced is covered for 90 days.
