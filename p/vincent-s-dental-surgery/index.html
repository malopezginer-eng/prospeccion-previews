What we do

Dental implants

Titanium roots placed to carry a single tooth, a bridge or a denture. The clinic's main area of work, planned with digital CT scanning and backed by a Master's in dental implantology.

Braces and orthodontics

Straightening for teeth that are crowded, gapped or out of line. The second thing the practice is known for, alongside implants.

Check-ups, scaling and fillings

Routine cleaning and polishing, fillings, extractions and wisdom tooth removal. The everyday visits that keep the bigger work from being needed.

Root canal, crowns and bridges

Saving a tooth that is infected or badly broken down, then rebuilding it with a crown, a bridge or a porcelain veneer.

Laser treatment

Three laser systems on site (diode, Er:YAG and Nd:YAG) used to disinfect root canals and gums, calm sensitive teeth, reduce swelling, whiten and treat snoring.

Facial aesthetics

Muscle relaxant and hyaluronic acid injections for frown lines, a heavy jaw line, or volume lost around the mouth after extractions. Deliberately minimally invasive; filler results last around six months.

About Vincent's Dental Surgery

Vincent's Dental Surgery opened in 1998 and has stayed on the same stretch of Tanjong Katong Road ever since, in a conservation building a short walk from the Katong food strip. Dakota and Paya Lebar MRT are both nearby, and a long list of buses stops outside Tanjong Katong Girls' School.

Dr Vincent holds a BDS from Singapore and a Master of Science in dental implantology from the University of Warwick, and implants and braces are where most of the clinic's work sits. The surgery runs its own digital CT scanner and three laser systems, so planning and treatment happen in one place. Marianne, the hygienist, has been treating adults and children since 2006, and patients tend to name her and Dr Vincent by name when they talk about the practice.
