About Studio Fluff

Studio Fluff is a small pet salon in the shops at 607 Ang Mo Kio Avenue 4, a short walk from the flats it serves. It grooms dogs, cats and rabbits, and keeps a retail corner with food, toys and accessories so you can pick things up while you wait.

The salon is cage-free and works by appointment only, so pets are not stacked up waiting their turn. Senior and sensitive animals are taken on, provided you tell the groomers about any medical condition beforehand. Weekday slots usually go one to two weeks ahead and weekends two to three, so message early.

Questions people ask

How do I book?

WhatsApp +65 8939 3631. First time, send your name, your pet's name, breed and weight, and a note on temperament.

How far ahead should I book?

One to two weeks for a weekday slot and two to three weeks for a weekend. Same-day is only possible if something opens up.

How often does my pet need grooming?

Roughly every two to four weeks for a basic groom, or every four to six weeks for a full groom with a trim.

My dog is old and has health problems. Will you still groom him?

Yes, but tell them about any medical condition before the appointment so they can adjust. Senior and sensitive grooming is done at the owner's discretion.
