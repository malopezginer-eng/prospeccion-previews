About The Flower Practice

The Flower Practice started in 2017 and is run by Jerrell Kwan and Wendy Sim from a studio at 23A Queens Close in Queenstown. It began partly to push back on the idea that floristry is not a man's trade, and the name is the point: they treat every arrangement as practice, something to get better at rather than a formula to repeat.

Orders are taken by email and through the shop on their site, with delivery island-wide at a fixed rate. Fresh flower orders need at least three days' notice so stems can be sourced for that specific job. Wedding work is quoted as a package with set-up and delivery included, and the final price moves with which blooms are in season.

Questions people ask

How much notice do you need?

At least three days for anything with fresh flowers. Dried and preserved pieces can usually move faster.

What does a wedding package cost?

The simple package runs about S$450 to S$600, the grand package S$1,100 to S$1,400 and the premium package S$1,900 to S$2,500. Set-up and delivery are included; the final figure depends on which flowers are available.

How do I pay?

Visa, Mastercard and American Express, or PayPal.

Can I cancel an order?

Cancellations are looked at case by case and carry a S$20 admin charge. Once an order has been made up or handed to the courier it can no longer be cancelled.
